FAQ

Is Sit Tight free?

Yes. Version 1 has no in-app purchases. It contains ads (paced interstitials) and optional rewarded hints.

What is Dual Exit?

A mode where you clear two marked cars to two exits on one 6×6 board — not just the amber hero.

What is the Daily Jigsaw?

A 12-piece car still that resets every day. Separate from the sliding campaign lots.
