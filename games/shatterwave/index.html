Shatterwave: Music Shooter

Shatterwave is a neon music shooter where the walls pulse to the beat and bosses wait. The arena is the song. Drag to steer your ship; it fires on its own. Stay off the glowing waveform walls, because touching one costs health.

Every shape your lasers hit chips apart into pixels, and every level ends at a boss built from hundreds of them. Shoot away its armor, hit the glowing weak points and break it apart. Each new level runs a little faster with a fresh color palette.

  • Four weapon patterns. Start with Single center and earn px to unlock the other three.
  • Hangar. Spend px on ships and shot patterns, and pick colors for your ship.
  • Continue and Double. Shattered? Watch an optional ad to Continue, or to double the px you just earned.
  • Daily. A new daily boss, the same for every player.
  • Challenge a friend. Send your score and see if they can beat it.
