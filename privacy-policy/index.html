Privacy Policy

Sit Tight is published by AP Finish Carpentry LLC, doing business as TrueSquare Digital (“we”). Contact: support@truesquaredigital.com. Last updated: 2 September 2026. This policy must match the Play Data safety form.

What the game is

Sit Tight is a single-player puzzle. There are no accounts and no in-app purchases in version 1.

Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

The Android build shows ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect device identifiers (including Advertising ID), app interactions, diagnostic data, and other data described in AdMob’s Play data disclosure and Google’s ads privacy documentation. We do not sell your information as a separate data-broker product. Personalized ads depend on your consent and platform settings.

In the European Economic Area, UK, and Switzerland we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (a Google-certified consent manager) before ads load where required. You can reopen privacy options from the title screen. US state “sale/share” signals are handled through AdMob Privacy & messaging.

What we collect directly

We do not run our own analytics or crash SDK in version 1. Play Console / Android Vitals may receive crash reports from the platform. Support email you send us is stored in our mailbox so we can reply.

Children

Sit Tight is not directed at children under 13. The art is charcoal and amber rather than a kids’ toy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, email support and we will delete our mailbox copy.

Retention and deletion

We keep support emails as long as needed to resolve the request and meet legal bookkeeping. We do not keep a player profile. Google’s ad systems are controlled through Google’s tools and the in-app privacy options.

If we add sign-in, analytics, or a crash SDK, we will update this page and the Data safety form before that binary ships.

Website

This website is a static marketing site. It does not set account cookies for login. Third-party embeds (for example YouTube or Google Fonts) may set their own cookies under their policies.
