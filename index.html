FAQ

Good to know

Is Sit Tight free?

Yes. Version 1 has no in-app purchases. It contains ads (paced interstitials) and optional rewarded hints.

What is Dual Exit?

A Sit Tight mode where you clear two marked cars to two exits on one 6x6 board, not just the amber hero.

Where can I get Amber Fire?

Amber Fire: Night Bubble Match is live on Google Play. Follow us on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok for updates.

Privacy and ads?

Ads run through Google AdMob. See our Privacy Policy. EEA, UK and Swiss players get a consent form where required.
