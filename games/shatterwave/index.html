Shatterwave: Music Shooter

Waveform walls on both sides of the arena move with the music, and when the track builds, the walls build with it. Every shape your lasers hit chips apart into pixels. A timed boss arrives in every level, and you will hear the pitch drop before you see it.

Drag to steer your ship; it fires on its own. Stay off the walls, hold out for the boss, then tap Challenge to send your score to a friend.
